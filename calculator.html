EMI calculator

A reducing-balance calculator. Early months contain more interest. Later months contain more principal. ₹5,00,000 at 12% a year for 60 months comes to about ₹11,122 a month before anyone adds a fee.

How to read the result

The monthly figure is principal plus interest, rounded to the nearest rupee. The last instalment is adjusted so the balance ends at zero. A processing fee, insurance, or GST is not inside that number.

Use the second rate when two lenders have quoted you. Use the extra-payment box only as a guide. Some loans allow part-payment after a lock-in, and some charge for it. The sanction letter is the rule.
