New and used cars

Car loan

For a specific vehicle. The car is usually hypothecated to the lender until you close the loan, and a down payment is normal.

Enquire about this loan Open full calculator

When this loan fits

A new car starts from the proforma invoice. A used car needs the registration certificate, insurance, seller details, and often a valuation. The loan amount is linked to the vehicle value, not only to your income, though the income still has to carry the EMI.

Dealership rate cards can hide a fee or a compulsory add-on. Compare the total you will pay, then decide.

A sensible range

Amount guide₹1 lakh – ₹50 lakh
Tenure guide12 – 84 months
Indicative rate8.75% – 16% a year

These bands are for a conversation. They are not a quote. A lender can offer a rate outside them.

Before you apply

  • Decide the on-road price and the amount you will put in yourself.
  • A personal loan can also fund a car, usually at a higher rate. Run both EMIs.
  • The lender's name is commonly noted on the RC until the loan ends.
  • Insurance has to stay valid. Some lenders package it. Ask what is included.

Also compare: Put the same amount into the personal loan calculator. Keep the option with the lower total payment, after fees.

Questions on this loan

Do you arrange both new and used cars?

We help with the loan on both. The used-car file needs the RC and a value the lender will accept.

Is the full on-road price financed?

Often you pay a margin. The exact share depends on the lender and whether the car is new or used.

Who owns the car during the loan?

You use it. The lender usually holds a hypothecation on the registration until the last EMI.
