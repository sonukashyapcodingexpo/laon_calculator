Terms

By using this website you can read about loans and send an enquiry. That use does not create a loan agreement.

Not an offer

Rates, amounts, and tenures are indicative bands for discussion. The EMI is an estimate. A bank, housing finance company, or NBFC decides whether to lend, at what rate, and with what fees.

Your information

If you apply to a lender, the information you give must be true. Prime Solution is not responsible for an application that hides an existing loan or a document.

Fees

Interest, processing fees, and other loan charges are the lender's, and they belong in the lender's documents. If Prime Solution would charge you a fee, that fee is explained before you agree. Prime Solution may receive a fee from a lender after a loan is disbursed.

No promise of approval or speed

Nothing on this website promises that a loan will be approved, or that money will arrive in a fixed number of minutes or days.

The lender's agreement

Once a loan is sanctioned, the lender's agreement controls repayment, part-payment, foreclosure, and security. Read it before you sign.
