Home loan

For the house itself: buying one, building on your land, adding a floor, or moving an existing home loan when the full cost of leaving is actually lower.

Enquire about this loan Open full calculator

When this loan fits

A home loan is usually the longest tenure and the lowest rate of the six products on this site. The property you are buying or building is the security. Construction loans are commonly released in stages against the progress of the work, not as one cheque on day one.

A balance transfer needs a cold comparison. Put the new EMI next to the charges for closing the old loan and any processing fee on the new one. A lower rate card is not automatically a lower cost.

A sensible range

Amount guide₹5 lakh – ₹5 crore
Tenure guide5 – 30 years
Indicative rate8.40% – 12.50% a year

These bands are for a conversation. They are not a quote. A lender can offer a rate outside them.

Before you apply

  • Keep the agreement, allotment letter, or construction estimate with the income papers.
  • Stamp duty and registration are often outside the loan amount. Confirm that before you count the money.
  • A co-applicant with income can change the eligible amount.
  • Your own contribution, if the lender asks for one, should be traceable in the bank.

Also compare: If the house is already yours and the money is for something else, the closer product is a loan against property.

Questions on this loan

Can you finance the stamp duty?

Many lenders do not. Ask before you treat registration money as part of the loan.

I am building on my own plot.

That can be a construction loan, released in stages. Bring the land papers and a cost estimate.

Is a balance transfer always worth it?

Only when the saving beats the cost of moving. We compare the total, not the headline rate alone.
