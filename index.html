Questions

Before you call

Do you give the loan yourself?

Prime Solution is a loan desk in Gaya. We help you understand the product and prepare an application. The bank, housing finance company, or NBFC decides the rate, the charges, and the approval.

Will this website reduce my credit score?

No. The calculator and the eligibility guide run in your browser. They do not call a credit bureau. A lender may check your credit when you actually apply.

Which loan should I open first?

Match it to the purpose. A home, or a property you already own, usually supports a larger amount at a lower rate. A personal loan needs no collateral, and the rate is often higher. If you are unsure, send the purpose on WhatsApp.

What does the EMI include?

Principal and interest only. A processing fee, insurance, or GST is extra. Those charges, if any, should be written in the lender's sanction letter.

Do I pay Prime Solution?

If any fee is payable by you, it is explained before you agree. Interest and processing charges on the loan itself belong to the lender. Prime Solution may receive a fee from a lender after a loan is disbursed.

What should I never send?

Do not send a card number, OTP, ATM PIN, or net-banking password. A genuine loan enquiry does not need them.
