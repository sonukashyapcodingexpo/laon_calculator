Personal loan

For a cost that belongs to the household: treatment, a wedding, college fees, travel, or a repair. You are not asked to mortgage the house for a smaller need.

When this loan fits

A personal loan is borrowed on income and credit history. Because there is usually no collateral, the rate sits above a home loan or a loan against property. That can still be the right product when the amount is moderate and you want a simpler file.

Say the purpose in plain words. A lender uses it, and so do we, to check that a personal loan is cleaner than a business or property loan.

A sensible range

Amount guide₹50,000 – ₹40 lakh
Tenure guide12 – 60 months
Indicative rate10.50% – 24% a year

These bands are for a conversation. They are not a quote. A lender can offer a rate outside them.

Before you apply

  • Most lenders look for an age roughly between 21 and 60, steady income, and a credit history they can read.
  • Salaried applicants are usually asked for salary slips. Self-employed applicants are usually asked for income tax returns.
  • The money is not tied to a shop invoice, but the EMI still has to fit beside the EMIs you already pay.
  • We do not promise same-day credit. The lender's timeline is the one that counts.

Also compare: If you own a house and the amount is large, also open the loan against property. The EMI is often lower.

Questions on this loan

Is a personal loan secured?

Usually no. That is why the rate is often higher than a home loan or a loan against property.

Can it cover a wedding or a hospital bill?

Yes. Those are common reasons. Name the purpose when you enquire so the file matches the need.

What decides the amount?

The guide on this page is ₹50,000 to ₹40 lakh. The lender sets the real limit from income, existing EMIs, and credit history.
